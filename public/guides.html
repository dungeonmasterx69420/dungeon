Setting up

Two apps cover everything: Stremio for films and series, Jellyfin for live TV. Both take a couple of minutes, and the accounts are already made.

Find your login

Your DungeonStream page holds the Stremio email and password we made for you.

Sign in

Tap Log in in Stremio and enter them. The catalogue is already loaded onto the account, so films and shows are there straight away.

On iPhone: Stremio runs from Safari, and playback needs a free external player. Install VLC, Outplayer or Infuse, then in Stremio go to your profile picture → Settings → External Player and pick it. The iPhone guide has the whole thing with pictures.

Sign in

Your DungeonCast username and password are on your DungeonCast page.

Signing in on a TV. Already signed in on your phone? On the TV pick Quick Connect, then type the code it shows into the phone app under Settings → Quick Connect. No password typing with a remote.
